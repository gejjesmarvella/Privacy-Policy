Privacy Policy

Gejje’s Marvella – Plastic Surgery & Dermatology · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Gejje’s Marvella (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or submit an enquiry form (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, city, and email address (if you provide one).
  • Enquiry details: the service you are interested in (for example, hair transplant), your preferred clinic, preferred call time, and any message you send us.
  • Technical details: basic information from our website and ad platforms, such as device type, pages visited, and which ad you responded to.

Please do not share detailed medical records or sensitive health information through enquiry forms. Clinical information is collected only during a consultation.

2. How we use your information

  • To call, message, or email you about your enquiry and to schedule or confirm consultations.
  • To answer your questions about our treatments and services.
  • To improve our services, website, and advertising.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Lead forms on Facebook and Instagram

When you submit an instant form on Facebook or Instagram, the details you enter are shared with us by Meta Platforms. Meta handles your information under its own privacy policy. We use the details only to respond to your enquiry.

4. Sharing of information

We share your information only with:

  • Our clinic team members who need it to assist you.
  • Service providers who help us operate (for example, messaging, scheduling, or IT services), who are required to protect your data.
  • Authorities, where required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond to you and for legitimate business or legal purposes. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your data protected.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to withdraw your consent to be contacted, or to raise a grievance about how your data is handled. To do so, contact us using the details below, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Our enquiry forms are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian’s consent.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its revised date.
